My story

Why I built this

Growing up in Buffalo Grove, I watched families in our community — neighbors, classmates' parents, people at the library — struggle to find affordable medical care. Not because the resources didn't exist, but because nobody had put them all in one place in a way that was easy to find, easy to trust, and available in the languages people actually speak.

"A family shouldn't have to spend hours searching the internet to find out where they can get a flu shot for free, or whether the clinic down the street accepts uninsured patients."

I'm a sophomore at Adlai E. Stevenson High School, Class of 2027, and I have a deep interest in medicine and public health. I started noticing this gap — between the resources that exist in Lake County and the people who need them — and I wanted to do something about it that was actually useful, not just a school project that gets filed away.

So I built this. A free, searchable, bilingual directory of 30+ verified health resources across Lake County — free clinics, mental health services, food pantries, dental care, immunization sites, and senior services. Every listing has been verified by phone. Every one includes the languages spoken, the hours, and whether you need insurance. And it's available in English, Spanish, and Polish, because those are the three most spoken languages in our county.

This site has no ads, no login, no cost to the people who use it. It is maintained by a high school student who believes that access to health information is a right, not a privilege — and that a teenager with a laptop can do something real about it.

How it works

How listings are verified

Unlike a Google search or a general database, every resource on this site has been personally checked. Here's the process:

1

Phone verification

Every organization is called directly to confirm their current address, phone number, hours, languages spoken, and whether they accept uninsured or Medicaid patients. If nobody answers after two attempts, the listing is held until confirmed.

2

Quarterly re-verification

Hours change. Phone numbers change. Organizations close or move. Every listing is re-verified by phone every three months and the "Last verified" date is updated. Stale listings are flagged or removed.

3

Community submissions reviewed within 7 days

Anyone can suggest a resource using the form below. Every submission is reviewed, called, and added within one week if it meets the criteria — free or low-cost, serving Lake County residents, currently operating.

4

No paid listings, ever

Organizations cannot pay to be listed or ranked higher. Inclusion is based solely on whether a resource is free or low-cost and genuinely serves Lake County families. This site has no advertisers and no commercial relationships.
